higgs-realtime  (wss://api.boson.ai/v1/realtime)
Used four distinct ways, and it is the backbone of the whole project.

1. The interviewer's voice. A persistent session that conducts the interview in English on
   the founder's behalf, one question per turn, adapting as it goes.

2. The interviewer's ears. The participant's answer is pushed back into that same session as
   raw audio (input_audio_buffer.append / commit) with no response generated — so the
   interviewer hears the actual delivery and never a transcript. This is what lets the
   follow-up react to HOW something was said. A speech-to-text pipeline cannot do this,
   because the delivery is gone before the agent sees the turn.

3. Tool calling, mid-turn. The interviewer calls lookup_competitor during the conversation to
   fetch the real current US price of whatever brand the participant just named, then asks
   them to choose between the two products at those real prices.

4. Text modality (output_modalities: ["text"]). Writes the founder's report — the verdict,
   the recommended actions, and the honest limits — from the measured prosody values. It is
   never allowed to invent a number, only to interpret ones we computed.

It also plays the simulated respondent when running without a live participant (main.py with
no --live), so the whole interview is two Higgs Realtime sessions passing audio to each other.

Measured latency to first audio across a full run: 460–930 ms.


higgs-stt-3.1  (POST /v1/audio/transcriptions)
Transcribes the live human participant. Deliberately off the conversational path — it runs
only so the founder's report can quote what was said. The interviewer itself never sees its
output.
