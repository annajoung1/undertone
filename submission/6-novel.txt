Prosody is a control signal here, not a dashboard. The measurement changes what the agent does next, inside the same conversation: the words stay positive, the delivery falls below that speaker's own norm, and the interviewer asks a harder question on its own.

Making that honest was the hard part.

Absolute thresholds fail. A 75 is not a low score. It is only a signal against a 96 baseline from the same person, so every answer is normalized to the speaker rather than to a scale.

A single axis fails too. We measured Higgs TTS going flat on pitch range (15.1 to 7.5 semitones) and a real human going flat on speaking rate (2.07 to 1.21 words per second) while their pitch range actually rose. People flatten differently, so any axis falling 25% below their own peak counts.

The browser nearly killed it. autoGainControl and noiseSuppression normalize loudness and dynamics, erasing the exact signal being measured. Audio is captured completely raw.

Two Higgs Realtime sessions pass PCM directly to each other, and tool calls fire mid-turn against real US prices.

Two separate surfaces, because a participant who can see their own score stops being a participant.
