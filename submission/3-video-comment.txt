UNDERTONE
An AI runs US customer interviews for Korean founders who can't run them themselves,
and reads how people said things — not just what they said.


WHAT YOU'RE WATCHING

Two screens, deliberately separated.

LEFT — the US participant. A plain interview. No scores, no meters, nothing measured on
screen. They should never know their delivery is being read, because knowing would change it.

RIGHT — the founder. Empty until the interview ends, then it fills itself in.


WHY THIS NEEDS HIGGS, SPECIFICALLY

The interviewer never receives a transcript. It hears the raw audio from Higgs Realtime and
picks its next question from that.

That distinction is the whole project. In an STT -> LLM -> TTS pipeline, delivery is gone
before the agent ever sees the turn — it can only react to words. Ours reacts to how the
words landed.

Everything on screen runs on Boson:
  - higgs-realtime — the interviewer's speech, and its ears
  - higgs-realtime — tool calls fired mid-turn to look up real competitor prices
  - higgs-realtime — Korean brief in, English interview out, one model, no translation layer
  - higgs-stt-3.1  — transcription, afterwards, for the founder's report only

Measured latency across this run: 460–930ms. Any slower and the follow-up stops being a
conversation and becomes a survey.


WHAT'S NEW

Prosody is the control signal, not a readout on the side.

Pitch range, speaking rate and pause ratio are measured off every answer and compared against
that speaker's own baseline — never an absolute scale. A 75 isn't a low score. It's only a
signal because that person normally sounds like a 96.

When the words stay positive and the delivery falls below their own norm, Undertone asks a
harder, more specific question on its own. The honest answer comes out of the follow-up.


HOW IT'S BUILT

Python orchestrator, two Higgs Realtime sessions passing audio to each other, prosody measured
from raw 24kHz PCM with numpy (autocorrelation F0, adaptive silence threshold). The browser
captures the participant with every audio enhancement switched off — autoGainControl
normalises loudness and erases the exact signal we're measuring.

The repo includes the real artifacts from this run, including two recordings of the same
sentence said two ways. Same words, 75 vs 96. Listen to them.


HONEST LIMITS

One participant is not a sample. This doesn't replace research — it kills seven bad messages
before you spend $50k finding out.

The participant here is me, answering live. In production they're a real consumer who was
sent the product.
